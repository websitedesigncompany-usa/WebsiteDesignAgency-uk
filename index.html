Questions

Website Design Company — frequently asked questions

The questions we are asked most often by US businesses planning a website.

How much does a website cost?
Our website design packages start at $199 for a 5-page starter site and scale to $3,999+ for complex platforms. Every package is a fixed price with a published feature list — not an hourly meter. Most US businesses land between $399 and $1,199.
How long will my website take?
A starter site ships in 5–7 business days. Business and e-commerce builds run 10–21 days. Custom web applications are scoped individually, typically 4–10 weeks. We commit to a delivery date in the written scope before work starts.
Do I really get unlimited revisions?
Yes. Unlimited revisions are included on the design direction you choose. We work in structured review rounds so your feedback lands in one consolidated pass rather than trickling in over weeks, which is what actually makes the promise workable.
Will my website be mobile-friendly and fast?
Every build is mobile-first and designed at 360px upward. We set an explicit performance budget — LCP under 2.5 seconds on 4G, CLS under 0.1 — and verify on real device sizes rather than a desktop preview scaled down.
Is SEO included or is it an upsell?
SEO fundamentals are included in every build: unique title and description per page, proper heading hierarchy, canonical URLs, Open Graph tags, structured data, XML sitemap, robots.txt, image alt text and Search Console setup. Ongoing SEO campaigns are separate because they are ongoing work.
Who owns the website when it is finished?
You do — completely. Source code, design files, content, domain registration and hosting credentials all transfer to you on final payment. We include a documented handover call and training session. There is no lock-in and no hostage situation.
What do I need to provide?
Your logo files if you have them, any brand guidelines, your content or a rough idea of your content, and a clear picture of who you are trying to reach. If you do not have content ready, we can help structure it — that is part of the scope on most packages.
Can you redesign my site without losing my rankings?
Yes. We map every existing URL, preserve redirect chains, keep title and heading structure where it already performs, and roll out in stages so rankings do not dip during the transition. Several of our projects have gained traffic immediately after a redesign.
What happens after launch?
You get 30 days of post-launch support as standard, and longer support windows on higher tiers. After that you are free to maintain it yourself — we provide a CMS, a written guide and a training session — or keep it on a care plan if you would rather we handled updates, security patching and monitoring.
Do you work with businesses outside the USA?
Yes. We serve clients in 72 countries including the UK, Canada, Australia, the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Pakistan, India, Germany, France and Brazil. We price in your local currency and write copy in your market's language rather than exporting a US template.

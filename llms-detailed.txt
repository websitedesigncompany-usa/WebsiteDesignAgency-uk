Every build is mobile-first and designed at 360px upward. We set an explicit performance budget — LCP under 2.5 seconds on 4G, CLS under 0.1 — and verify on real device sizes rather than a desktop preview scaled down.

SEO fundamentals are included in every build: unique title and description per page, proper heading hierarchy, canonical URLs, Open Graph tags, structured data, XML sitemap, robots.txt, image alt text and Search Console setup. Ongoing SEO campaigns are separate because they are ongoing work.

You do — completely. Source code, design files, content, domain registration and hosting credentials all transfer to you on final payment. We include a documented handover call and training session. There is no lock-in and no hostage situation.

### Can you redesign my site without losing my rankings?
Yes. We map every existing URL, preserve redirect chains, keep title and heading structure where it already performs, and roll out in stages so rankings do not dip during the transition. Several of our projects have gained traffic immediately after a redesign.

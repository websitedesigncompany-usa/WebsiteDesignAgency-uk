MVP Development Services

Six things that make the difference between a site that ships and a site that works. Here is how we approach mvp development services.

Custom work, not a template assembly line

Every build is designed from a blank file against your brief and your market. We do not buy a theme, swap the logo and resell it. That is why our mvp development services looks like your business rather than the last eleven clients.

Mobile-first, and tested on real devices

The majority of traffic is on a phone, so mobile is the primary design target rather than a shrunk-down desktop layout. We verify at 360px up, and we check on real hardware — not just a browser preview at desktop width.

Speed treated as a design constraint

We set an explicit performance budget on day one: LCP under 2.5 seconds on 4G, CLS under 0.1, images in WebP with lazy loading below the fold, and no render-blocking JavaScript. Speed is a feature, not an afterthought we optimise for later.

SEO built in, not bolted on

Every page ships with a unique title and description, a proper heading hierarchy, canonical URLs, Open Graph tags, and the right structured data for the page type. You are not paying extra to retrofit search fundamentals.

A CMS your team can actually use

You get an admin panel with sensible fields, plus a recorded walkthrough and a written guide. Publishing a page, post or product should never require opening a ticket.

Unlimited revisions

We work in structured review rounds so your feedback lands in one consolidated pass. Revisions on the selected design direction are unlimited — we would rather get it right than get it signed off quickly.

Fixed price, published inclusions

Packages start at $2,499 and the feature list is written out in full on this page. No vague "and other services" line items, no surprise change orders halfway through.

You own everything at handover

Code, design files, content, domain and hosting credentials transfer to you on final payment. We include a documented handover call so your team knows exactly how everything works.

Concrete deliverables, written down

Specifically what you receive: riskiest-assumption scoping workshop, clickable prototype before any code, functional build for the core user journey, clean, documented, extensible codebase, plus everything listed in the package table above. If it is not on the list, we do not charge you for it.

How it works

Our mvp development process, step by step

No black boxes. You always know what is happening, what we need from you, and what happens next.

  1. 1

    Brief and scope (Day 0–1)

    You fill in the form on this page or book a call. We come back within one business day with questions, a recommended package, a fixed price and a delivery date. MVP Development Services does not start until you approve that scope in writing.

  2. 2

    Research and wireframes (Day 1–4)

    We analyse your market, your closest competitors and the search demand behind this service in United States. You get a sitemap and low-fidelity wireframes for every template, so structure is agreed before anyone spends time on visual design.

  3. 3

    Design and unlimited revisions (Day 4–10)

    You see a first design pass, then structured review rounds with unlimited revisions until the direction is right. We do not start building until you approve a design — this is where most projects go wrong when they skip it.

  4. 4

    Build, CMS and SEO wiring (Day 10–16)

    We build responsive, accessible and fast, wire up the CMS, add on-page SEO, structured data, analytics and form routing, and run a full QA pass across devices, browsers and screen readers where relevant.

  5. 5

    Launch and aftercare (Day 16+)

    We handle DNS, SSL, redirects, sitemap submission and search console verification, then hand over source access, documentation and a training session. Thirty days of post-launch support are included as standard.

MVP Development Services — frequently asked questions

Straight answers to the questions we are asked most. If yours is not here, ask us directly.

What exactly is an MVP?
The smallest version of the product that produces real evidence about whether people want it. Not a broken full product — a working slice of one, built well enough to learn from.
How do you decide what to leave out?
We start from the assumption your business is most exposed to, and build only what tests it. Features that do not affect that answer are explicitly out of scope and we tell you why.
Will the code survive the next version?
Yes. We build on a real architecture with tests, not a prototype scaffold. The difference is that when you scale, you extend the codebase rather than rewrite it.
Do you do equity or revenue share?
No. We invoice fixed fees like every other engagement so there is a clear commercial relationship and a clear deliverable.
